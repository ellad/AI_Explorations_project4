# Midnight Magician assets

These are AI-generated assets and preserved drafts for the second story. The runtime uses the approved standalone performer poses, rabbit and pawprints listed below. The Developer approved the visual direction, composition and motion on a physical phone.

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool on October 24, 2026. The existing `composite_reference.png` fairground artwork was supplied as a style, palette and lighting reference. No external copyrighted asset was added.

## Files

- `magician-components-red-draft.png` — superseded saturated-red color test, preserved for comparison.
- `magician-components-charcoal-draft.png` — charcoal and burgundy revision with reduced rabbit brightness.
- `magician-components-charcoal-v2-draft.png` — current review version with a further-dimmed rabbit and pawprints.
- `curtain-components-draft.png` — separated plum-and-orange curtain panels.
- `stage-interior-draft.png` — isolated empty stage/interior concept. It includes more exterior tent structure than requested and requires review before use.
- `magician-poses-v3.png` — current integrated transparent three-pose sheet: intact hat-worn entrance, hat flourish and full-body bow. Generated from the charcoal character direction, then corrected to close the inherited shoulder sockets and keep both arms attached in every pose.
- `magician-entrance-v4.png`, `magician-flourish-v4.png`, and `magician-bow-v4.png` — standalone transparent runtime poses generated from the v3 character direction. These replace SVG viewBox crops so pixels from adjacent poses cannot leak into the scene.
- `spectral-bunny-v2.png` — current integrated standalone transparent rabbit. Generated from the approved dim smoky rabbit direction without neighboring magician parts, symbols or pawprints.
- `bunny-pawprints-v2.png` — current integrated standalone transparent four-print trail, generated without the red costume fragments present in the old shared-sheet crop.

All PNGs report an alpha channel. Runtime integration uses three standalone performer poses and the standalone rabbit directly. The prior full-stage and curtain rasters remain documented drafts but are no longer used at runtime; the existing painted tent and curtains remain visible without a replacement overlay. The rabbit and pawprints receive an additional brightness/saturation reduction in CSS.

## Prompt record

The asset prompts requested:

1. A transparent component sheet matching the fairground's textured storybook style, containing a tall featureless shadow magician, detachable gloved arm, detachable top hat, spectral rabbit and four pawprints, all separated and uncropped.
2. A targeted correction removing the hat from the magician's body and reducing broad background haze while preserving the component layout.
3. A red outfit exploration, followed by the Developer-directed revision to a predominantly charcoal-gray suit with burgundy-red lapels, vest, lining, cuffs, hat band and feather. Amber rim light, ivory glove/cravat and gold hardware remain.
4. Two further rabbit corrections reducing the bright white core and broad violet bloom toward a dim smoky gray-lavender apparition with a tight edge glow.
5. Two separate worn plum curtain panels with burnt-orange lining and rope ties, designed to translate apart.
6. An empty, dark plum circus stage with a worn wood floor and centered amber spotlight, isolated from the surrounding scene.

Every prompt prohibited text, labels, gore, weapons, extra characters and watermarks, and requested genuine transparency outside the components.
