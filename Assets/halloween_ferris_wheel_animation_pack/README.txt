HALLOWEEN FERRIS WHEEL CART SPRITES

cart_01.png through cart_03.png: three complete illustrated cart variants cropped from a generated sprite sheet. All are 300x400 transparent RGBA PNGs.
cart_position_01.png through cart_position_08.png: eight individually addressable cart assets (repeating the three variants).
animation_preview.html: Open in a browser to view a rotating Ferris wheel whose carts stay upright.
animated_wheel.svg: self-contained animated wheel illustration embedding cart images.

The sprites are new illustrated assets, NOT cutouts with missing wheel pieces. They are designed for compositing but are not perfect pixel matches for the original background wheel. To animate inside the original fairground, hide or replace the old static wheel layer, and position this animated wheel behind the entrance.
